\chapter{March}

\section*{March 2nd}
The class started with the story "Malala's Magic Pencil". The story was about how Malala was a girl that liked to draw and study, and after noticing the problems of Pakistan she started to dream about helping to solve the problems of her people like war and extremism. The story ended showing how her writings were important in bringing worldwide attention to the situation of her people.  

I believe the professor chose the story to represent the importance of proper writing and how powerful good texts can be. This relates to the current subject of good academic writing, because good academic productions can provide small advancements to human knowledge and science as a whole. Therefore like Malala's writing, good academic writing can provide change and contribute to a better world, even though only by a small amount.  
After the story there was a round of quick introductions among classmates, and then we discussed the course plan, going through the evaluation process and the topics that will be covered by the classes.  

It seems the classes will be interesting. After all there will be a good emphasis on how different English is from Portuguese writing-wise, beyond only translations, but the logic itself, especially when dealing with summaries.  

The idea of improving my writing and seeing this language that feels so familiar to me through new lenses excites me. Judging by the reading of the children's book, I believe that we will read quite a bit and get familiar with different texts and learn good argumentation and sentence writing in the language in a highly technical and objective perspective.
