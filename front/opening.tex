\chapter{Opening Text}

\section*{Introduction}
This portfolio intends to provide a general view of my experience and reflections upon the Academic Writing class taught by professor Cyntia Bailer. It should be a source of information about the class, its materials and ideas that were brought to me with it. Besides a collection of topics from the class, it will contain my personal observations and thoughts about what we've learned during class.

\section*{Who Am I?}
I am Gustavo Guerreiro, a computer science (CS) student at FURB. I am already at the end of the course. All mandatory subjects are done. Now I just need to work on the final project and get some elective courses from other courses. However, I am going for a double diploma in the University of Jade in Germany, so I have to briefly pause my undergraduate studies on the specific area and study subjects such as Academic Writing and "Direito Civil - Parte Geral" in order to prepare for my studies over there.

I consider myself to be a curious and multidisciplinary person. I usually like to read and learn about topics outside my CS degree like philosophy, theology and mathematics (of course, such studies are not formal in any way, shape, or form, I just cherry pick the topics that interest me the most and read about them).

\section*{Expectations}
This course interests me quite a bit. It is a new way of thinking, talking about writing structures and techniques, maybe a bit of neurology and philosophy of language excites me. I am always up for learning how to improve myself, and writing is such an important part of expression. It makes me look forward to the classes.

I have little background both on writing long English texts and writing academic texts in general. Although I am familiar with the day-to-day use of the language through my job, I hope to learn more about the language, its structure, and improve my capacity of providing arguments and writing clearly in this language.
